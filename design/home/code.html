Why we are here

Three simple promises

01

We roast our own

Small batch coffee roasted twice a week right behind our counter. You can smell the chaff and crackle from the pavement.

02

The kitchen bakes daily on site

Sourdough, laminates, and pastries out of the oven every single morning. When today’s bake sells out, that’s genuinely it until tomorrow.

03

Stay as long as you like

A quiet wooden table, soft morning light, and no rush. Ever. Bring your book or notebook; nobody will hover over your empty cup.
